class robocasa.environments.kitchen.multi_stage.chopping_food.arrange_cutting_fruits.ArrangeCuttingFruits(*args, **kwargs)#

Bases: Kitchen

Arrange Cutting Fruits: composite task for Chopping Food activity.

Simulates the task of selecting fruits from the fridge and placing them on the cutting board while leaving non-fruit items in the fridge.

Steps:

  1. Select and place the 2 fruits from the fridge on the cutting board

  2. Leave the 2 non-fruit items in the fridge
