  1. Pull the toaster lever down and wait for the toaster to finish toasting the bread.

class robocasa.environments.kitchen.multi_stage.toasting_bread.toast_one_slot_pair.ToastOneSlotPair(*args, **kwargs)#

Bases: Kitchen

Toast One Slot Pair: composite task for Toasting Bread activity.

Simulates the task of troubleshooting a toaster with a broken slot-pair by moving bread to the working slot-pair and toasting both slices together.

Steps:

  1. Identify which slot-pair is broken and which is working

  2. Pick the bread slice from the broken slot-pair

  3. Place it in the working slot-pair alongside the existing bread slice

  4. Press the lever to toast both slices together
